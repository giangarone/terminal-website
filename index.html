Can I start with a free plan?

Yes. Free gives you core charts, personal watchlists, standard indicators, and limited AI assistance. Pro and Elite add deeper market data, more advanced tools, and expanded intelligence as your workflow grows.

Which markets can I trade?

PrimeTerminal brings crypto, stocks, futures, and commodities into one workspace. Connect supported exchanges and trading accounts to analyze your markets and manage trades from your charts.

What’s the difference between L1, L2, and full depth?

L1 covers top-of-book information, including the best bid, ask, and latest trade. L2 shows additional price levels and resting liquidity. Full depth extends that view across the available order book for a more complete picture of market structure. Pro includes NASDAQ and CME L1 and L2; Elite adds full-depth capabilities.

Can I create indicators without writing code?

Yes. Describe the indicator you have in mind in natural language and let AI build it. Refine it, add it to your charts, and share or sell it through PrimeTerminal’s integrated marketplace.

Can I execute trades directly on a chart?

Pro and Elite support direct chart trading through connected trading accounts. Place orders, drag stop-losses and take-profits, set trailing stops, and make breakeven adjustments in the same workspace you use for analysis.

Are the prices on this page live?

Yes. The Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana spot prices above come from Coinbase’s public market feed and refresh approximately every five seconds when prices change. The connection indicator shows whether the feed is live. These crypto quotes are separate from the NASDAQ and CME data available inside PrimeTerminal.
