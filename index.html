    Your questions.
    Answered.

    Can I start with a free plan?

    Yes. You can start for free and trade from your charts with a connected account. Our most powerful features — deeper market data, advanced AI, and more — are on premium plans.

    Which markets can I trade?

    MarketStream brings crypto, US stocks and ETFs, forex, futures, and commodities into one workspace. Connect supported exchanges and trading accounts to analyze your markets and manage trades from your charts.

    What’s the difference between L1, L2, and full depth?

    L1 covers top-of-book information, including the best bid, ask, and latest trade. L2 shows additional price levels and resting liquidity. Full depth extends that view across the available order book for a more complete picture of market structure. Pro includes NASDAQ and CME L1 and L2; Elite adds full-depth capabilities.

    Can I create indicators without writing code?

    Yes. Describe the indicator you have in mind in natural language and let AI build it. Refine it, add it to your charts, and share or sell it through MarketStream’s integrated marketplace.

    Can I execute trades directly on a chart?

    Yes. Trade directly on the chart through connected trading accounts. Place orders, drag stop-losses and take-profits, set trailing stops, and make breakeven adjustments in the same workspace you use for analysis.
